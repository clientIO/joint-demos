# Journey Board — Product Context

## What it is

A collaborative customer journey mapping tool (the TheyDo/UXPressia
category). Cross-functional teams co-map the customer lifecycle as
touchpoints and flows across fixed funnel stages, then run a seeded cohort
of customers through the map to see conversion and where drop-off
concentrates — at the handoffs between departments.

## Who it is for

Developers evaluating JointJS+ (`@joint/react-plus`) for building real-time
multiplayer diagramming products — shown through a tool a business
stakeholder recognizes in three seconds.

## Register

Product. The dress of a serious SaaS planning tool (Linear/Figma-grade
density and calm), not a dashboard and not a toy.

## Strategic principles

1. **Presence is the org chart.** Who-does-what is answered by the artifact:
   every card carries its owning department's color, cursors are labeled
   "Sales · Anna", and the activity feed reads like meeting minutes.
2. **The business insight is the collaboration.** Customers leak between
   departments; the cohort run quantifies it ("62% of losses right after a
   handoff") — which is exactly why the tool is multiplayer.
3. **Rooms are small and mortal.** A board seats at most 8 departments,
   the lobby lists boards in flight, and an untouched board retires after
   30 minutes — no unbounded rooms, no zombie channels.
4. **Alive with two tabs, zero setup.** Without a Liveblocks key a board
   runs over BroadcastChannel; with `VITE_LIVEBLOCKS_PUBLIC_KEY` it goes
   online unchanged.

## Anti-references

- Generic flowchart-with-cursors demos; whiteboard toys.
- Dashboard chrome: KPI tiles, gauges, gradient cards.
- The other JointJS demos (workflow builder, data modeling, mermaid,
  electrical designer, knowledge graph) — no duplication.

## Tone

Plain, competent, quietly confident — the voice of a tool that runs the
Monday funnel review.
